SMS / Mobile Messaging Terms

Last updated: [DATE]

These SMS Terms apply to anyone who opts in to receive text messages from Pro Casa LLC ("Pro Casa," "we," "us," or "our").

Program Name

Pro Casa SMS Program

Program Description

By opting in, you agree to receive text messages from Pro Casa related to your property inquiry, appointment reminders, transaction updates, customer service, and requested information. If you separately opted in to marketing messages, you may also receive promotional messages, property updates, and follow-up communications.

How to Opt In

You may opt in to receive SMS messages from Pro Casa by:

  • Submitting our contact form at /contact and checking the SMS consent box(es)
  • Verbally consenting during a phone call after our representative reads the same consent disclosure
  • Texting START or a similar opt-in keyword to a number we have provided

SMS consent is never required as a condition of doing business with us.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your interaction with us. You may receive transactional messages tied to your inquiry as well as occasional marketing messages, if you have opted in to marketing.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

How to Opt Out

You may cancel SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message we send. After you send STOP, we may send one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. After that confirmation, you will not receive any further SMS messages from us unless you opt in again.
