About Pro Casa

Real estate is rarely just about real estate.

Behind every distressed property is a person — or a family — going through something difficult. We started Pro Casa because we saw too many homeowners pushed into bad outcomes by agents who couldn't help them, attorneys who cost too much, and wholesalers who treated them like a transaction.

Our Approach

Pro Casa is a direct real estate buyer based in South Carolina. We purchase homes for cash from homeowners facing situations the traditional market isn't built for — foreclosure, probate, tax liens, divorce, code violations, problem tenants, inherited properties, and other complex circumstances.

Because we buy directly with our own funds, we don't depend on bank financing, appraisals, or buyer contingencies. That's why we can close in as little as seven days when speed is what you need — and why we can take a property exactly as it sits, with no repairs and no clean-out required from you.

What We Believe

Honesty over pressure.

If selling to us isn't the right move for you, we'll tell you. We'd rather lose a deal than push someone into the wrong decision.

Speed when it matters.

Foreclosure auctions don't wait. Probate timelines don't wait. We move at the speed your situation requires.

Privacy by default.

No yard signs. No MLS. No open houses. The neighbors don't need to know your business — and they won't from us.

Clean transactions.

We use a licensed title company on every deal. Funds are wired through escrow. Everything is documented and above board.

Service Area

We primarily purchase properties in South Carolina and surrounding markets. If you're outside that area, contact us anyway — we may still be able to help or refer you to a trusted buyer in your region.
