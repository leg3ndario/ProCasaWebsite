## ⚡ QUICK REFERENCE

### Before Deployment

**Required replacements in ALL HTML files:**
- `[Business Mailing Address]` → Your street address
- `[City, State Zip]` → Your city/state/ZIP
- `[Phone]` → Your phone number
- `[Email]` → Your email address
- `[DATE]` → Today's date (in legal pages)
- `[STATE]` → Your state (in Terms)
- `[COUNTY]` → Your county (in Terms)
- `[Timezone]` → Your timezone (in Contact page)

**If your legal business name is NOT "Pro Casa LLC":**
- Replace ALL instances of "Pro Casa LLC" with your actual legal entity name

## ⚠️ CRITICAL A2P REQUIREMENTS

Your website MUST have these to pass A2P approval:

### 1. Contact Form (`contact.html`)
- ✅ Two separate SMS consent checkboxes
- ✅ Both optional (NOT required)
- ✅ Both unchecked by default
- ✅ Form works without checking either
- ✅ Each label includes: business name, message types, frequency, rates, HELP, STOP

### 2. Privacy Policy (`privacy-policy.html`)
- ✅ Contains exact paragraph: "No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes..."
- ✅ Does NOT say you sell/share mobile data

### 3. Terms & Conditions (`terms.html`)
- ✅ Section 6: SMS/Mobile Messaging Terms
- ✅ Includes: Program Name, STOP, HELP, frequency, rates

### 4. Footer (all pages)
- ✅ Links to Privacy Policy, Terms, SMS Terms
- ✅ Business address, phone, email visible

✅ **All of these are already built into this website!**
